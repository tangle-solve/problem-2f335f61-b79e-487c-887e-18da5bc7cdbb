### CHEM-000496: Reliable density-functional double excitations
- **Subfield:** Theoretical and computational chemistry
- **Open:** Predict excitation energies, oscillator strengths and potential-energy surfaces for states with substantial double-excitation character across diverse molecules, including state mixing and bond dissociation.
- **Status:** Ordinary adiabatic linear-response TDDFT has a longstanding limitation here. A 2025 ensemble-functional construction resolved representative failures; broad validation and reliable dynamics remain open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Double excitations participate in photochemistry, relaxation and multiexciton generation.
- **Key works:** T. Gould, S. G. Dale, L. Kronik and S. Pittalis (2025), *State-Specific Density Functionals for Excited States via a Density-Driven Correlation Model*, [DOI:10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.228001](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.228001).
